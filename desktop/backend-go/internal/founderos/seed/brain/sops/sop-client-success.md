# Service active clients

## Purpose

Cadence, deliverables and renewals on rails.

## Owner

[[client-success]] — one worker, one job (monogamous by design).
Runs on: builtin · recall + plaud + slack.

## Trigger

Kicks off when it is time to "run the weekly check-in cadence per client, no skipped weeks" — on cadence or on the upstream event, whichever lands first.

## Steps

1. Run the weekly check-in cadence per client, no skipped weeks
2. Track deliverables against the sold scope and flag slippage early
3. Log Recall call notes back to the client record the same day
4. Score account health monthly: green, watch, or at risk with a reason
5. Raise renewals and upsell openings 30 days out to Sasha and Sales

## Definition of done

The run is complete when "raise renewals and upsell openings 30 days out to sasha and sales" has verifiably happened and is logged to the run history.

## Escalation

If any step fails twice in a row, or the data looks wrong, stop and escalate to Sasha Bell ([[person-sasha]]). Never fake a green run.
