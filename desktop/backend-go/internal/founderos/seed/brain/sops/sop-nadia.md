# Set content strategy & approve drops

## Purpose

The human editorial gate on everything published.

## Owner

[[person-nadia]] — one worker, one job (monogamous by design).
Runs on: human · judgment call.

## Trigger

Kicks off when it is time to "review last cycle’s performance numbers from the dashboard" — on cadence or on the upstream event, whichever lands first.

## Steps

1. Review last cycle’s performance numbers from the dashboard
2. Set this week’s angles and slot them on the calendar
3. Approve or kill every queued asset before it publishes
4. Spot-check published posts landed exactly as approved
5. Debrief the crew on what worked and what died

## Definition of done

The run is complete when "debrief the crew on what worked and what died" has verifiably happened and is logged to the run history.

## Escalation

If any step fails twice in a row, or the data looks wrong, stop and escalate to the operator. Never fake a green run.
