# Close the books monthly

## Purpose

The human sign-off on every month’s numbers.

## Owner

[[person-dana]] — one worker, one job (monogamous by design).
Runs on: human · judgment call.

## Trigger

Kicks off when it is time to "import bank and processor statements for the month by the 3rd" — on cadence or on the upstream event, whichever lands first.

## Steps

1. Import bank and processor statements for the month by the 3rd
2. Categorize transactions using the statement’s own categories
3. Reconcile against the income the agents recorded and chase every gap
4. Confirm refunds and disputes are reflected in the venture totals
5. Deliver the month-end P&L to Alex with three lines of commentary

## Definition of done

The run is complete when "deliver the month-end p&l to alex with three lines of commentary" has verifiably happened and is logged to the run history.

## Escalation

If any step fails twice in a row, or the data looks wrong, stop and escalate to the operator. Never fake a green run.
